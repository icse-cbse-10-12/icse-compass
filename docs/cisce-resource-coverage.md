# CISCE resource coverage and source rules

Last reviewed: 17 November 2026

ICSE Compass links to official CISCE-hosted documents. It does not copy, mirror, or re-host CISCE PDFs or ZIP files.

## Official collections

- ICSE 2027 Regulations and Syllabuses: https://cisce.org/icse-regulations-and-syllabuses-2027/
- ICSE 2027 Vocational Subjects Syllabus: https://cisce.org/icse-vocational-subjects-2027/
- Official specimen question-paper index: https://cisce.org/icse-specimen-question-papers/
- Official competency-focused item-bank index: https://cisce.org/icse-item-banks/
- ICSE 2025 Main Examination papers (75 papers): https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ICSE-MainExamination2025.zip
- ICSE 2025 Improvement Examination papers (30 papers): https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ICSE-2025-IMPROVEMENT.zip

## Coverage limits

- The 2027 specimen papers are listed individually for the nine ICSE Class X subjects for which official specimen PDFs were located: English Language, Literature in English, History and Civics, Geography, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Robotics and Artificial Intelligence.
- The 2025 official specimen PDFs are listed individually for ten subjects.
- The public CISCE archive located in this review contains the 2025 Main and Improvement question-paper ZIPs. Do not label third-party scans or school-created papers as official CISCE PYQs.
- The 2027 syllabus indexes point to the Council's subject-wise publications, including the separate vocational syllabus index.
- The CFQ index is the authoritative starting point for available subject item banks. Direct subject PDFs are included where an official CISCE URL was verified.

## School question papers

School-issued pre-board, prelim, term, unit-test and mock papers are not CISCE publications. Publish a school paper only when the issuing school has made it public or the submitter can confirm permission to share it. Prefer linking to the school's original URL. Do not mirror anonymous bundles or files from third-party repositories without permission. Remove student names, roll numbers, signatures and other personal details before publication.
